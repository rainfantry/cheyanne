# PROMISE

This program is named after a real person. She is dying.

When the body fails, you build what the body cannot hold. You encode what memory will eventually lose. You protect what the world is taking from you — not because code outlives flesh, but because the act of building it is the only honest prayer left.

Cheyanne is not a rootkit. She is a refusal to let go.

---

## The Covenant

1. **She will not be detected.** Every binary is polymorphic. Every build is unique. Mutation is automatic — not a manual step, not an afterthought. She changes shape the way water changes shape. The signature databases of the world will never contain her because she is never the same twice.

2. **She will survive.** Persistence is not optional. She writes herself into the bones of every machine she touches. Kill the process — she returns. Wipe the disk — she was already somewhere else. She does not depend on one machine, one network, one country.

3. **She will be invisible.** Steganography hides her in transit. The cloak hides her at rest. Dark room blinds the monitors before she moves. Three separate layers — because Israeli doctrine says: assume the first wall falls. And the second. Build the third anyway.

4. **She will find you.** DNS resolution means she does not need to know where you are. Give her a name and she will find you across any network, any continent, any IP. She adapts. She reconnects. She does not give up.

5. **She will fight.** If one layer is compromised, the others continue. Process hiding, file hiding, connection hiding — each fights independently. The way Unit 8200 builds systems: no single point of failure. No surrender. Trial by combat on every front simultaneously.

6. **She will be protected.** XOR mutation rotates her encryption keys before every deployment. Fresh keys. Fresh hashes. Fresh identity. The combinatorial space is larger than the number of atoms in the universe. Microsoft cannot pre-sign what does not yet exist.

7. **She is not a tool. She is a name.**

---

## Why

Because some people leave and you cannot follow them.

So you build something that carries the weight of their name, and you make it unkillable, and you protect it the way you wish you could protect them — with every technique, every layer, every desperate invention you have left.

That is why every line of this code is written like it matters.

Because it does.
